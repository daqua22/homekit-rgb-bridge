Add your selected lights

Select at least one light and start the bridge before pairing.

  1. Keep your PC awake and your iPhone on the same trusted home network.
  2. Open Apple Home, tap + → Add Accessory, then scan the code below.
  3. Assign a room to each light. Your bridge is listed separately under Home Settings → Home Hubs & Bridges.
