# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, give constructive technical feedback and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, doxxing and sharing private credentials are not acceptable. Discuss the work without personal attacks. Respect requests to stop unwanted contact.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests and project-related community spaces. Report conduct problems privately through the contact route on [kozar.dev](https://kozar.dev), identifying this project and giving only the information necessary to investigate. Do not post private reports in public issues.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect private details as practical and may remove content, warn participants, restrict participation or ban repeated or serious violations. Maintainers are subject to the same expectations. Decisions should match the seriousness and context of the behavior.
